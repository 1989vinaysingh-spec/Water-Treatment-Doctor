Water Treatment Doctor V18.5.4 — Smart Design Fix Pass
Date: 2026-09-06

Fixed:
1. User-entered HRT now overrides the biological/main reactor HRT for STP/ETP/CETP/MBR/MBBR/ASP/SBR instead of only appearing as a separate overall value.
   Example: STP 100 KLD, HRT 10 hr -> Biological Reactor basis 10 hr and design volume 45.8 m3 including 10% allowance.
2. Total listed tank HRT and retention volumes now recalculate from the actual tank-wise HRT values after the override.
3. Dosing pump sizing no longer incorrectly uses plant hydraulic flow (m3/hr or KLD). It now states that dosing pump capacity must come from chemical dose, product strength/density and run time and be selected in L/hr or mL/min.
4. Sludge pumps no longer inherit raw plant hydraulic flow. Duty is tied to sludge production, concentration, cycle and head.
5. Equipment matching corrected so text such as 'Bio media' cannot be incorrectly treated as EDI/membrane equipment due to substring matching.
6. Pump, filter, membrane, clarifier, blower/diffuser and panel sizing guidance made service-specific.
7. Smart Design tables improved for mobile: horizontal scrolling, sensible minimum column widths, and no letter-by-letter word breaking.
8. Full P&ID Drawing button contrast fixed for visibility.

Engineering note:
Outputs remain preliminary design aids. Final sizing must be verified using actual inlet/outlet analysis, loading/mass balance, hydraulic profile, process kinetics, OEM data, civil/structural criteria, applicable standards and site requirements.
