Water Treatment Doctor Privacy Policy

App version 18.4.4 • Developer: Vinay Kumar Singh

English

Water Treatment Doctor is a field-reference and calculation application for water and wastewater treatment professionals.

  • Photos and videos: Media selected for AI diagnosis is processed only after the user chooses AI analysis. The media or sampled video frames are sent to the AI backend URL configured in the app.
  • AI backend: The backend operator must use a secure HTTPS connection and publish its own applicable privacy/retention terms. Do not enter confidential plant information unless your organization permits it.
  • Local data: App settings and daily-log data may be stored locally on the device using browser/WebView storage.
  • Camera/gallery: The app can invoke Android system camera/gallery pickers so the user can choose plant photos or videos.
  • No automatic upload: The app does not intentionally upload media merely because it is selected; AI transmission occurs when the user starts AI analysis.
  • Engineering safety: AI and calculators provide decision support only. Plant readings, OEM manuals, approved SOPs, SDS, permits and competent personnel remain authoritative.

हिंदी

Water Treatment Doctor पानी और wastewater treatment professionals के लिए field-reference और calculation app है।

  • फोटो/वीडियो: AI diagnosis के लिए चुनी गई media तभी AI backend को भेजी जाती है जब user AI analysis शुरू करता है।
  • AI backend: Backend को सुरक्षित HTTPS connection और अपनी privacy/retention policy का पालन करना चाहिए।
  • लोकल डेटा: App settings और daily log की जानकारी device पर local storage में रह सकती है।
  • कैमरा/गैलरी: App Android system picker/camera के माध्यम से user को फोटो/वीडियो चुनने देता है।
  • सुरक्षा: AI और calculators केवल सहायता के लिए हैं; वास्तविक plant readings, OEM manuals, approved SOP, SDS और site safety rules को प्राथमिकता दें।

For Play Store publication, host this same policy on a public HTTPS webpage and paste that public URL into Play Console.
